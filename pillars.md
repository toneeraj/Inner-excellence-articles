# The pillars

The framework this writing works inside. Every article declares one of these
in its front matter, and `render.py` refuses to build one that names anything
else — so the site, the README and the pillar rail cannot drift apart, and a
pillar with no article yet still shows up rather than quietly disappearing.

Order here is the order everywhere. The paragraph under each heading is the
gloss a first-time reader gets.

## P1 — The Default Setting

Everyone runs on an automatic self-centered lens. Not a moral failing —
factory configuration. You are the center of your own experience, immediate
and vivid, and everyone else is peripheral. The lens was never chosen, which
is exactly why it goes unexamined.

## P2 — This Is Water

The most obvious, important realities are the hardest to see, precisely
because they are ambient. Two young fish swim past an older fish who asks how
the water is. They swim on a while, and then one of them asks: what is water?

## P3 — Attention Is a Moral Act

Freedom is not the absence of tedium. It is what you do inside it. The
checkout line, the traffic, the third status meeting — these are not the
interruptions before real life. They are where the choice actually lives,
because that is where the default setting is loudest.

## P4 — Choose What You Worship

There is no such thing as not worshipping. Everybody worships; the only
choice is what. Most of the defaults — money, intellect, being seen as
competent, the body — will eat you alive, because they never deliver enough.

## The movement — effort to look, no effort in the seeing

Not a pillar but the shape the practice takes. Turning to examine your own
default read takes deliberate effort; nobody drifts into it. The recognition
itself cannot be forced, and straining at it is more self. Effort to look,
no effort in the seeing.
