---
title: Let us look at this one, not at this kind
pillar: The movement — effort to look, no effort in the seeing
originally_sent: 2026-10-26
standfirst: Dogen says to study the self, which means the one instance that just went through you, not the account of what kind of thing it was.
sources:
  - Dogen, Genjokoan, translated by Aitken and Tanahashi.
---

Something small lands on you and it is gone inside a second. A flare of heat at a tone of voice. A small drop when someone else does well. In that second almost all of us reach for the name of it. Dogen, a thirteenth-century Zen teacher in Japan, has a line about what gets studied, and what he names is nearer in than the name. Let's take his first link.

> To study the buddha way is to study the self. To study the self is to forget the self. To forget the self is to be actualized by myriad things.
>
> — Dogen, Genjokoan

To study the way is to study the self. The way is not studied by studying the way. There is no second subject sitting somewhere with its own chapters, waiting to be got through. What there is to work on is whatever just went through you, and that is the only instance of it you will ever get from the inside.

The account of a thing is portable and the thing itself is not. Two seconds of heat about a dishwasher cannot be held, repeated, carried into tomorrow or told to anyone. A sentence about why you were short-tempered can be all four. So the swap feels like getting something rather than losing something, and it is made in well under a minute.

There are three things a person can do with the thing that just landed, and each one costs something. He can go and find out about it: read on irritation, learn where envy comes from, get the mechanism straight. He comes away with a sound account of that kind of thing, and the one instance he had has gone cold while he was looking it up. He can leave the whole business alone and stop reading about himself. Then nothing ever turns to look, because nobody examines his own first read by accident, and Dogen's word is study, not stop studying. Or he can take the instance as the material. That costs him the dignity of the subject. There is no syllabus, nothing to tell him whether any of it is landing, and what he is looking at is two seconds of heat about a dishwasher.

So what are we aiming at. We want to study the one we had, and leave the kind of thing it was to look after itself.

*Let us see where this shows up in an ordinary day. Three places.*

- A tone of voice at home goes through you, and a second later you are explaining to yourself that you are tired.
- Someone tells you their good news, something drops slightly, and you begin accounting for the drop.
- You notice you have been rehearsing a conversation that has not happened, and you reach straight for why you do that.

One thing to watch in all three: the moment the particular turns into a kind. One question, two answers. Am I looking at what happened, or at an account of what happened.

You are not deciding anything in that second, and you are not trying to stop yourself explaining. Watch which of the two you are holding, and leave it as it is.
